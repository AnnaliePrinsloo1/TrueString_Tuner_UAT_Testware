## Defect Report: BUG-MOB-01

### 1. Summary information
* **Defect ID:** BUG-MOB-01
* **Title:** TrueString Tuner fails to detect E2, A2 and D3 synthetic frequency targets during standard tuning.
* **Date Logged:** 2026-06-01
* **Reporter:** Annalie Prinsloo
* **Status:** Closed (Rejected / Invalid)
* **Target Fix Version:** Version 6.0.10

### 2. Classifications
* **Defect Type:** Functional - Incorrect behaviour / Logic Error.
* **Severity:** Major
* **Priority:** High
* **Reproducibility:** Consistent

### 3. Traceability & Environment
* **Build/Software Version:** Version 6.0.9 Beta
* **Hardware/Devices tested:** 3x Test devices (Samsung Galaxy S25, Android 16, Samsung Galaxy S21, Android 16 & Samsung Galaxy A53, Android 16)
* **Traced To:** `TCOND-PT-01`
* **Test environment:** QA Environment (Using Szynalski Online Tone Generator as external input source)

### 4. Description & Steps to reproduce
* **Description:** The TrueString Tuner App fails to detect the three lowest synthetic frequencies (E2, A2, and D3) when using the standard tuning configuration. The three highest frequencies (G3, B3, and E4) are detected correctly. Severity was assessed as Major given that core pitch-detection functionality was failing for half of the required guitar string frequencies at the time of logging; Priority was assessed as High given the risk this posed to the app's primary use case ahead of release.

* **Steps to Reproduce:**
  1. Open the TrueString Tuner App
  2. Navigate to the **Settings** menu.
  3. Set the **Reference Frequency** to **440Hz**.
  4. Select **Standard Tuning** under the Tuning option.
  5. Navigate back to the main **Tuner** screen.
  6. Open the Szynalski tone generator website on an external device.
  7. Input the **E2** frequency (**82.4Hz**) and play the tone for 3 seconds.
  8. Repeat step 7 sequentially for the remaining frequencies: **A2** (**110.00Hz**); **D3** (**146.83Hz**), **G3** (**196.00Hz**); **B3** (**246.94Hz**) and **E4** (**329.63Hz**).

### 5. Test Results
* **Expected Result:** The TrueString Tuner App successfully detects all 6 synthetic frequencies played during Standard tuning.
* **Actual Result:** The app fails to detect the lower frequencies (82.41 Hz [E2], 110.00 Hz [A2], and 146.83 Hz [D3]) across all 3 test devices. The remaining frequencies (196.00 Hz [G3], 246.94 Hz [B3], and 329.63 Hz [E4]) are detected correctly.

### 6. Evidence & Attachments
* **File Attached:** `BUG-MOB-01_Frequency_Detection_Failure.mp4` (Screen recording showing the tone generator playing the frequencies and the tuner app failing to respond to E2, A2, and D3).
* **Annotations/Notes:** The issue appears to be isolated specifically to frequencies below 150 Hz.

### 7. Closure & Resolution
* **Status:** Closed/Rejected
* **Resolution Reason:** Not a bug - Environment issue
* **Closing comment:** Closed as Invalid. Retesting confirmed the TrueString Tuner App successfully detects E2, A2, and D3 frequencies when played on a physical acoustic guitar. The initial failure was an environmental artifact: the app's pitch-detection algorithm requires the harmonic overtones produced by real string vibrations, which are absent in the pure sine waves generated by the Szynalski website. No software defect exists. Testing methodology has been updated to use real instruments for future cycles.
